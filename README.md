# IW-Wohnindex Q2 2026 — Interactive Report

An interactive, visualization-first web version of a written report, created from the [Institut der deutschen Wirtschaft (IW)](https://www.iwkoeln.de) analysis. Not a replacement for the PDF, but a different path alongside it — both formats have their reasons to exist. Here, the reader moves through the content while animated charts bring the data to life, with a special focus on the visualizations themselves. Every figure also has a PNG download button, bridging the two formats from a single source.

This edition presents *IW-Wohnindex Q2 2026* (IW-Report 34/2026, 20.07.2026) by Pekka Sagner and Michael Voigtländer — hedonic price trends for German residential property, both purchase and rental. It is a demo rather than a full edition: it opens on Chapter 2, the report's first chapter carrying a figure, and shows that figure twice — finished as it appears in the report, then rebuilt series by series as the reader scrolls. The introduction, the regional and city breakdowns and the offer-count special section are deliberately not part of it. Brand derivation and the chart data digitization method are documented in `docs/iw-brand.md`.

Content and data are the IW's. This interactive version is an independent design study by Sandra Becker and is not endorsed by the Institut der deutschen Wirtschaft.

This report is built with my interactive-report template; the template itself (tech stack, project structure, how to add chapters and figures) is documented in the README on the `main` branch.
